About EasyFolio

Faculty need to keep a lot of records related to their academic work. Many of these records are repeated when faculty must submit information to different venues. EasyFolio App allows faculty to enter data once and then visually generate the requested report—resume, portfolio, or other records—automatically. Users log in through an accounting system and customize their own resume/portfolio components.

Technology

  • Front-end: Angular
  • Database: MongoDB
