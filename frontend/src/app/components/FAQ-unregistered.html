Frequently Asked Questions

Visual Portfolio—also known as EasyFolio—is a digital tool that helps users collect, organize, and generate academic or professional documents. Instead of entering the same information multiple times, users store their data once and quickly create resumes, CVs, portfolios, and other records using a visual, drag-and-drop builder.

The app is primarily designed for faculty, students, and professionals who need to manage academic or career-related records. It is especially helpful for individuals who frequently need to update resumes, maintain credentials, or prepare documentation for reviews, applications, or reporting purposes.

Users can create a variety of structured documents based on the information they store inside the app. This includes resumes, CVs, professional portfolios, certification documents, and other academic records. All items entered into the system can be visually arranged using templates or drag-and-drop tools.

You can generate several kinds of academic and professional documents, including:
  • Resumes

  • Professional or academic portfolios

Currently, Visual Portfolio supports exporting documents in:
  • PDF – for sharing and submitting finalized documents
  • DOCX – for further editing in Microsoft Word or Google Docs
Additional export options may be added in future versions based on user feedback.
