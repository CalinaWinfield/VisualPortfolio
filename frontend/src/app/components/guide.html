User Guide

  1. Login: Use your faculty credentials to log in to the EasyFolio App.
  2. Add Records: From the Dashboard, click “Items” to create or edit academic records such as publications or certifications.
  3. Upload Documents: Use “Document Builder” to upload and drag-and-drop components into a custom document.
  4. Generate Reports: Export your customized resume or portfolio in the desired format.
  5. Track Progress: Use “My Documents,” “To-Do,” and “Active Forms” sections to review saved files and task status.
  6. Log Out: Select “Log out” in the top navigation when finished.
